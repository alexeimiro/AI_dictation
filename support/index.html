Setting up the keyboard

  1. Open Murmur and follow the setup. It takes you to Settings when it's time.
  2. In Settings › Apps › Murmur › Keyboards, turn on Murmur and Allow Full Access.
  3. In any app, touch and hold the globe key and choose Murmur.

Why does Murmur open when I tap the mic?

iOS doesn't let keyboards use the microphone, so the Murmur app does the listening. The first tap, or the first after Murmur has been off for a while, opens Murmur, which starts listening and takes you back to your app. From an app Murmur can't reopen, like Safari, swipe right along the bottom edge of the screen to go back, as Murmur's Home says; the keyboard is listening when you get there. After that the microphone stays ready in the background, shown by the orange dot and Murmur's Live Activity, and each tap dictates without leaving your app. Choose how long it stays ready in Murmur › Settings › Turn off after.

Why does the keyboard need Full Access?

Only so the keyboard and the Murmur app can pass your dictation to each other. Murmur doesn't record or send what you type.

Murmur Pro

Murmur is free with 1,000 words a week, after a 14-day trial of Pro (one per iPhone). Murmur Pro has no weekly limit.

  • Subscribe: Menu › Upgrade to Pro.
  • New iPhone: sign in, then tap Restore purchase on the same screen.
  • Cancel: in iPhone Settings › Apple Account › Subscriptions. Apple manages subscriptions.
  • Refunds: Apple handles them, at reportaproblem.apple.com.

Invites

Share your invite from Menu › Invite a friend. A friend who enters your code within 14 days of creating their account gets a month of Murmur Pro, and you get a month once they finish their first dictation, up to 12 months in total.

Got a code from a friend? Open their invite link on your iPhone and tap Open in Murmur, or enter the code under Menu › Invite a friend.

Your data

Your history of transcripts and recordings stays on your iPhone. Your dictionary, snippets and notes are saved to your account so they're on all your devices. To write your text, each recording goes to OpenAI and its text to Anthropic; neither trains on them, and Murmur asks before the first one (you can change that in Settings › Data and privacy). The Privacy Policy has the details.

To delete your account and its data, open the menu, tap your account at the bottom, then Delete account. That doesn't cancel an App Store subscription.
