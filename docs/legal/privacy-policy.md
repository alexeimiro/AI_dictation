# Redmic Privacy Policy

Effective November 16, 2026

Redmic is a voice keyboard for iPhone made by OKNENOR L.L.C-FZ ("we"). This policy explains what
Redmic collects, why, and the choices you have. Questions: miralex13@gmail.com.

## What stays on your iPhone

- **Your transcripts** (dictation history) and **the recordings** Redmic keeps so you can play
  them back. You choose how long they're kept (Settings › Local data storage).
- **What you type** with the Redmic keyboard. The keyboard doesn't record or send keystrokes.

## What we collect and why

- **Your account:** your email address (or the private relay address Sign in with Apple gives
  us), your name if Apple or Google passes it on, and an account ID. They let you sign in and keep
  your plan and library on every device.
- **Your recordings and their text, to write what you say:** when you dictate, the audio goes
  from the Redmic app to our server and on to OpenAI, which turns it into text. When that text
  needs tidying or translating, it goes to Anthropic, whose Claude model rewrites it. Redmic asks
  for your permission before the first recording is sent, and you can take it back in Settings ›
  Data and privacy (Redmic then stops listening until you allow it again). Our server doesn't keep
  the audio or the text. OpenAI and Anthropic process them to return the result. Under their API
  terms neither uses them to train its models, and each may keep them for a limited time to detect
  abuse (OpenAI for up to 30 days).
- **Your dictionary, snippets and notes:** while you're signed in, they're saved to your account
  so they're on every device you use. They aren't shared with anyone.
- **Invites:** if you join with a friend's invite code, we record whose code it was, so you each
  get your month of Redmic Pro. Your friend sees how many people joined with their code, not who.
- **How much you dictate:** the number of words in each dictation, to apply the free plan's weekly
  limit, and what each dictation cost us to serve: how many seconds of audio and how much text the
  AI services handled, and which service did it. Not the words themselves.
- **Whether your iPhone has had a free trial:** Redmic Pro's free trial, and an invite's free
  month, is one per iPhone. To tell, Redmic asks Apple's DeviceCheck service, through our server,
  whether this iPhone has had one, and marks it when it gets one. Apple keeps that mark for us. It
  doesn't tell us who you are or give us an identifier for your iPhone, and we don't keep the token
  the app sends to ask.
- **Purchases:** if you subscribe to Redmic Pro, Apple tells us which plan you bought and when it
  renews, expires or is refunded, so we can grant Pro. We never see your payment details.
- **Your answers during setup:** what you plan to use Redmic for, how you heard about it, your
  languages and default style, your data-sharing choice and, if you ask for the link to Redmic for
  Mac and PC, the email address to send it to.
- **The emails we send you:** a welcome when you create your account, a few tips in your first
  two weeks, one question about how likely you are to recommend Redmic, and a reminder the day
  before a free trial ends. To write them we use your first name, your plan, and how much you've
  dictated (words, and on how many days), never what you said. We record which emails we sent.
  Each has an Unsubscribe link; sign-in codes still come whenever you ask for one. If you answer
  the question, we keep your score and anything you write with it.
- **Feedback and support messages:** what you write to us, with the app version, iOS version,
  device model and language, to look into the problem. Each message is also emailed to us, with
  your account's email address so we can reply. When you rate a dictation and have turned
  on "Improve Redmic for everyone", the rating can include that dictation's text.

We don't track you across apps and websites, show ads, or sell or rent your data.

## Who processes it for us

- **Supabase** hosts our server, database and sign-in, in the European Union (Frankfurt).
- **OpenAI** transcribes dictations.
- **Anthropic** tidies and translates the text of dictations.
- **Apple** handles Sign in with Apple, App Store purchases and the free-trial check
  (DeviceCheck). **Google** handles Sign in with Google.
- **Resend** sends our emails: sign-in codes, the emails described above, and your support
  messages to us.

Each processes data only to provide its service to us.

## How long we keep it

We keep your account data until you delete your account. Deleting it (Menu › Account › Delete
account) erases your account, library, usage and purchase records from our server, and revokes
Sign in with Apple. Two things stay:

- The records of what your dictations cost us to serve, no longer tied to your account.
- For 30 days, a scrambled fingerprint of the sign-in you used (not your email address or your
  Apple or Google ID itself), with how many words you had dictated that week and whether your
  free trial and invite month were used. It is there so that deleting an account and making a new
  one can't reset the free plan: if you sign up again within those 30 days, you carry on where you
  left off. After 30 days it is erased. You can ask us to erase it sooner.

Signing out deletes Redmic's data from that iPhone. Deleting the account doesn't cancel an App
Store subscription: manage that in Settings › Apple Account › Subscriptions.

## Your rights

You can see, correct, download or delete your data. Most of it is in the app; for anything else,
write to miralex13@gmail.com. Depending on where you live, you may also have the right to object to
processing, and to complain to your data protection authority.

## Children

Redmic isn't made for children under 13 (16 in the European Union), and we don't knowingly
collect their data.

## Changes

If this policy changes in a way that matters, we'll say so in the app before it takes effect.
