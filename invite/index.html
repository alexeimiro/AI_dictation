How it works

  1. Get Murmur on your iPhone and create your account.
  2. Open this page again on that iPhone and tap Open in Murmur, or enter the code in Murmur under Menu › Invite a friend.
  3. Enter it within 14 days of creating your account, and Murmur Pro is yours for a month. It counts as your iPhone’s free trial, so it works on an iPhone that hasn’t had one.
