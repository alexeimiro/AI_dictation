Talk instead of typing.

Murmur is a keyboard for iPhone that writes what you say. Switch to it in any text field, tap the mic and talk. Your words arrive punctuated, with “um” and “uh” gone, and with names spelled the way you spell them.

Coming soon to the App Store

What it does

  • Writes wherever you write. Messages, email, notes, documents and chats with AI.
  • Your words, or your words tidied. Word for word keeps exactly what you said. Improve my grammar fixes mistakes without changing your tone.
  • Translates as you speak. Speak one language and type in another.
  • Learns your words. A dictionary for names, brands and jargon, and snippets for what you type often.
  • Keeps your words yours. Your transcripts and recordings stay on your iPhone. Privacy Policy
