Talk instead of typing.

Murmur is a voice keyboard for iPhone. Tap the mic in any app, say what you mean, and Murmur types it in seconds: punctuated, with “um” and “uh” gone, and with names spelled the way you spell them.

Coming soon to the App Store.

What it does

  • Writes in any app. Messages, email, notes, documents and chats with AI.
  • Your words, or polished. Word for word keeps exactly what you said. Improve my grammar fixes mistakes without changing your tone.
  • Translates as you speak. Speak one language and type in another.
  • Spells your names. A personal dictionary for names, brands and jargon, and snippets for what you type often.
  • Private by design. Your transcripts and recordings stay on your iPhone. Privacy Policy
