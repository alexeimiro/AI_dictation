## Setting up the keyboard

1. Open Murmur and follow the setup. It takes you to Settings when it's time.
2. In Settings › Apps › Murmur › Keyboards, turn on Murmur and Allow Full Access.
3. In any app, touch and hold the globe key and choose Murmur.

## Why does Murmur open when I tap the mic?

iOS doesn't let keyboards use the microphone, so the Murmur app does the listening. The first tap,
or the first after Murmur has been off for a while, opens Murmur, which starts listening and takes
you back to your app. After that the microphone stays ready in the background, shown by the orange
dot and Murmur's Live Activity, and each tap dictates without leaving your app. Choose how long it
stays ready in Murmur › Settings › Disable Murmur after.

## Why does the keyboard need Full Access?

Only so the keyboard and the Murmur app can pass your dictation to each other. Murmur doesn't
record or send what you type.

## Murmur Pro

Murmur is free with 1,000 words a week, after a 14-day trial of Pro. Murmur Pro has no weekly limit.

- **Subscribe:** ☰ › Get unlimited words.
- **New iPhone:** sign in, then tap Restore purchase on the same screen.
- **Cancel:** in iPhone Settings › Apple Account › Subscriptions. Apple manages subscriptions.
- **Refunds:** Apple handles them, at [reportaproblem.apple.com](https://reportaproblem.apple.com).

## Invites

Share your invite from ☰ › Refer a friend. A friend who enters your code within 14 days of
creating their account gets a month of Murmur Pro, and you get a month once they finish their
first dictation, up to 12 months in total.

Got a code from a friend? Open their invite link on your iPhone and tap Open in Murmur, or enter
the code under ☰ › Refer a friend.

## Your data

Your transcripts and recordings stay on your iPhone. Your dictionary, snippets and notes are saved
to your account so they're on all your devices. The [Privacy Policy](../privacy/) has the details.

To delete your account and its data, open ☰ › Account › Delete account. That doesn't cancel an
App Store subscription.
